Project

Handy Man Services Management System

Full-stack web application built during the Codebegun and ExcelR internships

Problem

People struggle to find reliable, trusted service professionals and often face issues with the quality of their work. There is no organized platform to discover or book them.

My role

Designed and developed the system end to end. I architected the role-based database schemas, built secure RESTful APIs in Spring Boot with JWT authentication, implemented the dynamic registration, booking and attendance flows in React.js, and integrated SMTP-based email notifications across the booking lifecycle.

Solution

A three-role home-service booking platform for Admin, Customer and Service Partner, with:

  • Role-aware registration and JWT authentication
  • Real-time booking with automatic service-partner assignment
  • OTP-based service verification
  • SMTP-integrated email notifications across the booking lifecycle
  • A manual payment confirmation flow (Cash or UPI transaction ID)
  • An admin analytics dashboard with day, week, month and year order visualizations
